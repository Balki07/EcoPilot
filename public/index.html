    How EcoPilot generates your plan
    1. Your input — You describe your campus habits or sustainability concern in your own words.
    2. Optional profile context — If you have saved a profile, those preferences are included as background context (not verified facts).
    3. AI prompt processing — Your input and context are sent to an AI language model using a sustainability-focused prompt with fairness and ethics guidelines.
    4. AI-generated recommendations — The model returns a structured plan. Results are suggestions only and may not reflect your specific campus conditions, local policies, costs, or availability.
